- Loan period is 21 days; due date is borrow date + 21.
- Maximum 2 renewals per loan; a renewal sets the new due date to the renewal date + 21 days.
- Renewal is refused if anyone has a hold on the book, if the loan is already overdue, or if the renewal limit is reached.
- Fine is 25 cents per full day late, with no grace period, capped at 1000 cents per loan to avoid being harsh.
- Fines are added to the member's owed balance when the book is returned.
- A member who owes anything cannot borrow.
- Holds are only allowed on books currently on loan to someone else, and only one hold per member per book.
- Holds form a FIFO queue. When the book is returned, the first member in line gets it set aside with readyUntil = return date + 7 days.
- Only that member can borrow the book while it is set aside. If they miss the deadline, their hold is dropped and the next member gets 7 days starting the day after the previous deadline.
- Borrowing a set-aside book removes the member's hold.
- The set-aside member counts as position 1; readyUntil is non-null only for that member.
- payFine rejects non-integer, non-positive, or overpaying amounts (amount > owed), changing nothing.
- Duplicate or missing IDs in addBook and addMember fail; unknown members or books fail.
- Invalid date strings fail with ok:false.
- getMember for an unknown member returns an empty record rather than throwing.
- Loans are listed in borrow order, and holds in book insertion order.
- There is a single copy per bookId and no cap on the number of loans per member.
- Hold expiry is processed lazily at each call's date.
