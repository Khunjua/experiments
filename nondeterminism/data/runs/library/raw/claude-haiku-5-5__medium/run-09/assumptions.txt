- Loan period is 21 days; due date is borrow date plus 21 days.
- A renewal gives 21 more days counted from the renewal date, not from the old due date.
- A loan can be renewed at most 2 times.
- A renewal is refused if any member is waiting on the book, if the loan is already overdue, or if the 2-renewal limit is reached.
- Fines are 25 cents per day late, with a 3-day grace period; the first 3 days after the due date are free and fines start on day 4.
- Each loan's fine is capped at 1000 cents ($10).
- A fine is calculated and added to the member's balance when the book is returned; late returns are always accepted.
- A member who owes any money cannot borrow until they pay in full.
- Fines do not block renewals or holds.
- There is no limit on how many books a member can borrow or how many holds a member can have.
- A hold is allowed only when the book is on loan or is set aside for another member; placing a hold on a book that is simply on the shelf is refused (the member should borrow it instead).
- A member cannot hold a book they currently have on loan, and cannot place a second hold on the same book.
- The waiting line is first-come, first-served; position 1 is the member the book is currently set aside for, if any.
- When a book is returned and members are waiting, it is set aside for the first person in line with a pickup window of 7 days counting the return day (return day through return day plus 6).
- While a book is set aside, only that member can borrow it.
- If the pickup window lapses, that member is removed from the waiting line entirely (they lose their place), and the next person in line is set aside with a new 7-day window starting the day after the lapsed window ended. If nobody is waiting, the book goes back on the shelf.
- Lapses are applied based on the date of each call, so any call on or after the lapse date sees the lapse, regardless of how many calls happened in between.
- payFine accepts only a positive whole number of cents that does not exceed the amount owed; overpayments are refused.
- Member and book IDs must be non-empty strings; duplicate IDs are refused. There is no way to remove members or books.
- The date passed to payFine is not used, since fines are only calculated at return time.
- getMember for an unknown member returns empty loans and holds with an owed balance of 0.
- getMember lists loans and holds in the order the books were added to the library.
- A renewal on the due date itself is allowed; a renewal after the due date is refused.
- Dates are assumed to be valid 'YYYY-MM-DD' strings and are not validated.
