- Loan period is 21 days from the borrow date; borrowing is unlimited per member.
- A renewal sets the new due date to the renewal date plus 21 days.
- A member may renew at most 2 times per loan.
- Renewal is refused if the loan is already overdue (renewal date after due date).
- Renewal is refused if any hold is waiting on the book.
- Late fees are free for the first 3 days past due; after that, 25 cents per day for each day late beyond those 3 days.
- Late fees are capped at 1000 cents per loan.
- A late fee is charged at return time and added to the member's owed balance; overdue loans accrue nothing until returned.
- Holds can only be placed on books that are currently on loan.
- A member cannot place a hold on a book they currently have on loan.
- A member cannot place a duplicate hold on the same book.
- Hold position is the member's place in the line counting from 1; a set-aside member is position 1.
- When a book is returned and has waiting holds, the first member in line is set aside for a pickup window of 7 days starting on the return date.
- If the set-aside member does not pick up by the end of the window, they are removed and the next member is set aside for 7 days starting the day after the previous window ended.
- A set-aside book cannot be borrowed by anyone other than the set-aside member; when that member borrows it, their hold is consumed.
- Books with no waiting holds return to the shelf on return.
- Members with overdue loans or unpaid fines are not blocked from borrowing.
- Payments must be positive integer cents not exceeding the amount owed; partial payments are allowed; overpayment is rejected.
- Adding a duplicate book or member, or passing a null or undefined id, returns { ok: false }.
- Unknown members return an empty getMember result; unknown books or wrong-member actions return { ok: false }.
- Returning or renewing a book the member does not have on loan returns { ok: false }.
- Holds and pickup-window expiry are applied lazily based on the date of each call, which is correct because calls arrive in time order.
