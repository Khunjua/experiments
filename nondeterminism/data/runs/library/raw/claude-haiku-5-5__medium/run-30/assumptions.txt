- Loan period is 21 days from the borrow date.
- Each bookId is a single physical copy.
- A member may renew at most 2 times per loan.
- A renewal sets the new due date to the renewal date plus 21 days.
- Renewal is refused if the loan is already overdue (date after due date).
- Renewal is refused if any hold is waiting on the book.
- Fines have a 3-day grace period: returns up to 3 days late are free.
- Beyond the grace period, the fine is 25 cents for each full day late beyond the 3 grace days.
- Each fine is capped at 1000 cents (10 dollars).
- A fine is added to the member's owed balance at the time of return.
- A member may hold at most 5 books on loan at once.
- Members with 1000 cents or more owed cannot borrow, renew, or place holds; they can still return books and pay fines.
- A hold can be placed only when the book is on loan or already set aside for another member.
- A member cannot place a hold on a book they currently have on loan, or one they are already waiting for.
- The member at position 1 is the one the book is set aside for, and is still counted in the queue.
- When a book is returned with a waiting queue, it is set aside for position 1 for 3 days, counting the return date as day 1.
- The pickup window is inclusive of its last day.
- A set-aside book cannot be borrowed by anyone except the member it is set aside for.
- Borrowing a set-aside book removes that member from the queue.
- If a set-aside window lapses unclaimed, that member is dropped and the next waiting member is set aside starting the day after the lapsed window ended, for another 3 days.
- Lapses are processed lazily at the start of every method call that carries a date.
- payFine rejects non-integer, non-positive, or over-balance amounts; partial payments are allowed.
- getMember for an unknown member returns an empty structure instead of ok: false.
- Methods with an invalid date string (not a real YYYY-MM-DD calendar date) return ok: false; returnBook returns fine: 0 in that case.
- Duplicate addBook or addMember calls return ok: false.
- getMember lists loans in the order the books were first added.
- Returning a book requires that the member is the one who currently has it on loan.
- Returning a book is allowed even when the member is blocked by an outstanding balance.
