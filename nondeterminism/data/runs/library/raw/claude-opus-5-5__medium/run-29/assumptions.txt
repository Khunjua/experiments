- Each bookId is a single physical copy; adding an existing bookId or memberId returns ok:false.
- Unknown members or books, invalid IDs, or invalid dates return ok:false.
- The loan period is 21 days, so the due date is the borrow date plus 21 days.
- "A couple of times" means at most 2 renewals per loan.
- A renewal sets the due date to the renewal date plus 21 days.
- A renewal is refused if anyone has a hold on the book.
- A renewal is refused if the loan is already overdue.
- To be lenient, there is a 2-day grace period: returning up to 2 days late costs nothing.
- Returning more than 2 days late is charged 25 cents for every late day, including the grace days.
- The fine for a single return is capped at 1000 cents.
- A member may have at most 5 books on loan at once.
- A member cannot borrow while owing 500 cents or more.
- Owing money does not block renewals, holds or returns.
- A hold can be placed only when the book is on loan or already has a waiting line.
- A member cannot place a hold on a book they currently have.
- A member cannot place a second hold on the same book.
- When a book is returned and has holds, it is set aside for the first person in line. They have 7 days to pick it up, counting the return day, so readyUntil is the return date plus 6 days.
- If a set-aside hold is not picked up by readyUntil, it is cancelled the next day. The book is then set aside for the next person, for 7 days from the day after the previous expiry.
- Hold expiry is checked lazily whenever a method with a date is called.
- While a book is set aside, only that member can borrow it. Borrowing it removes their hold.
- Positions are counted from 1, and a set-aside hold has position 1.
- Payments must be positive integers no greater than the amount owed; overpayment returns ok:false.
- getMember lists loans in borrow order and holds in the order they were placed.
- getMember for an unknown member or with an invalid date returns { ok: false, loans: [], owed: 0, holds: [] } and does not throw.
- Successful getMember results include ok: true.
- A return must be made by the member who borrowed the book; otherwise it returns ok:false.
- Members cannot cancel holds, because the interface has no method for it.
