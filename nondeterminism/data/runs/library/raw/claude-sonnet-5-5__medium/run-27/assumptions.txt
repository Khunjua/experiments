- Loan period is 21 days; the due date is the borrow date + 21.
- A member may renew at most 2 times per loan.
- A renewal sets the new due date to the renewal date + 21 days, not the old due date + 21.
- Renewal is refused if anyone has a hold on the book, if the loan is already overdue (date after due date), or if the member does not have that book.
- A return on the due date is not late. Each day after the due date costs 25 cents.
- To be gentle, the fine is capped at 1000 cents per loan.
- A fine is added to the member's owed balance when the book is returned. Overdue loans do not accrue fines until they are returned.
- A member who owes any money cannot borrow.
- A member with an overdue loan cannot borrow.
- A member may hold at most 5 loans at once.
- Duplicate book or member ids are rejected by addBook and addMember.
- Invalid or unknown ids and malformed dates return ok:false.
- A hold can only be placed on a book that is currently out or set aside for another member. If the book is on the shelf, the hold is refused.
- A member cannot hold a book they currently have on loan, and cannot hold the same book twice.
- Hold queues are first-come, first-served. Position 1 is next, and the set-aside member is position 1.
- When a book with a queue is returned, it is set aside for the first member in line. They can pick it up through the last day of return date + 3.
- A book set aside for a member can only be borrowed by that member. Borrowing it removes their hold.
- If the pickup window passes unused, the hold is dropped. The next member in line gets a new 3-day window starting the day after the previous one ended. If nobody is waiting, the book returns to the shelf.
- Expiry is applied lazily at each call's date. A member's readyUntil is the last day they can pick up.
- payFine rejects non-integer, zero, negative, or overpaying amounts. It returns the remaining balance on success.
- getMember for an unknown member returns { ok: false }.
- getMember lists loans in borrow order, and holds in book-insertion order. readyUntil is null unless the member is first in line and the book is set aside.
- Holds on a book do not block returning it. Renewal is refused while a queue exists, even one that is waiting on a set-aside pickup.
