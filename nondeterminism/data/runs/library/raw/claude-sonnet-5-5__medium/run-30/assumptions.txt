- Loan period is 21 days; the due date is borrow date + 21.
- A loan can be renewed at most 2 times. Each renewal adds 21 days to the current due date.
- Renewal is refused if anyone has a hold on the book, if the loan is overdue (date after due date), or if the renewal limit is reached.
- A book returned on its due date or earlier has no fine. Late returns cost 25 cents per full day late, with no extra grace period.
- To avoid being harsh, the fine for one loan is capped at 2000 cents.
- Fines are charged at return time and added to the member's owed amount.
- Borrowing is refused while the member owes 500 cents or more. Smaller balances do not block borrowing.
- There is no limit on how many books a member can have out.
- Duplicate or missing ids in addBook/addMember fail. Unknown members or books fail. Invalid or malformed dates fail.
- A hold can only be placed on a book that is currently on loan or set aside for someone. Holds on a book on the shelf with nobody waiting are refused.
- A member cannot hold a book they currently have on loan, and cannot place a duplicate hold on the same book.
- Hold queues are first-come, first-served, and position is 1-based.
- When a book with holds is returned, it is set aside for the first holder. They can pick it up through the last day of return date + 7 days (readyUntil is inclusive).
- While a book is set aside, only that member can borrow it. Borrowing it removes their hold.
- If the pickup window passes, the hold is dropped. The next holder in line gets a window of 7 days starting from the previous readyUntil date (applied lazily at the next call).
- readyUntil is null for holds that are not yet set aside.
- Holders still waiting in line are not notified or charged anything. Expiry of an unclaimed hold carries no penalty.
- payFine rejects non-integer, zero, negative amounts and amounts above what is owed. It changes nothing in those cases.
- getMember for an unknown member returns { ok: false, loans: [], owed: 0, holds: [] }. For a known member it also includes ok: true.
- getMember lists loans in borrow order and holds in book-addition order.
- getMember applies hold expiry up to the supplied date.
