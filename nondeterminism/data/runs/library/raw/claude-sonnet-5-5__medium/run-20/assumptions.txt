- Loan period is 21 days; due date = borrow date + 21.
- A book can be renewed at most 2 times.
- A renewal extends from the current due date by 21 days, not from the renewal date.
- Renewal is refused if anyone has a hold on the book, if the loan is overdue (date after due date), or if the member does not have the book.
- Fine is 25 cents per full day late, charged when the book is returned and added to owed. A return on the due date costs nothing.
- To avoid being harsh, the fine is capped at 1000 cents per loan.
- Overdue books that are not yet returned accrue no fine until return.
- A member who owes 500 cents or more cannot borrow.
- A member with any overdue loan cannot borrow.
- A member can have at most 10 loans at once.
- Duplicate or missing ids on addBook/addMember fail.
- Unknown members or books, and invalid dates, fail.
- A hold can only be placed on a book that is on loan or set aside for someone else. Holds on available books fail.
- A member cannot hold a book they currently have on loan, and cannot hold the same book twice.
- The hold queue is first come, first served. Position 1 is next, including the member the book is set aside for.
- When a book with a queue is returned, it is set aside for the first holder, with readyUntil = return date + 7 days (inclusive).
- A set-aside book can only be borrowed by that holder. Borrowing removes their hold.
- If the holder does not pick up by readyUntil, the hold expires. The next in line gets readyUntil = previous readyUntil + 7, or the book becomes available if no one is waiting.
- Expiry is processed lazily on each call using the call's date, so a hold is still valid on its readyUntil day and gone the day after.
- payFine fails for non-positive, non-integer, or overpaying amounts (amount greater than owed). Nothing changes in that case.
- Borrowing a book that is on loan, including one's own, fails.
- Returning a book not borrowed by that member fails.
- getMember for an unknown member returns empty loans and holds and owed 0.
- getMember lists holds in book insertion order.
- readyUntil is null for holds that are not set aside.
