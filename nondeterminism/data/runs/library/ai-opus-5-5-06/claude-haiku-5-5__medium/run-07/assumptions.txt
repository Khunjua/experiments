- Arguments that are arrays are treated as non-objects and rejected with INVALID_INPUT.
- Malformed arguments (bad ID, bad date format, bad amount) are rejected before the clock is read, so they do not move the clock.
- A valid, non-earlier date moves the clock and runs hold expirations even when the call is then rejected for a missing member, a missing book, or a business rule.
- An amount that is not a safe integer of 1 or more is rejected with reason INVALID_INPUT; an overpayment is rejected with reason BAD_AMOUNT.
- Any exception raised while reading arguments is caught and returned as { ok: false, reason: 'INVALID_INPUT' } before any state changes.
- Hold expirations are applied to every book on each dated call, whether or not that call succeeds.
- IDs consisting only of whitespace are treated as non-empty and valid, since the spec says there is no trimming.
- Calendar dates from year 0000 to 9999 are accepted, and day arithmetic uses the proleptic Gregorian calendar via integer day numbers.
- Renewal is allowed on the due date itself (date on or before the due date is accepted).
- The loan creation counter and the hold placement counter share one sequence, which is used only to order results.
- A renewal keeps the loan's creation sequence number, as the spec requires.
- Fines are computed from the current due date, which includes any renewals already applied.
- Owed balances are not capped, and a member can place holds or borrow regardless of owed amount.
- A book freed with an empty line becomes available and has no readyUntil.
- getMember returns readyUntil only for a set-aside hold that belongs to the requested member.
- Rejection reason codes are taken from the suggested list in section 13 of the requirements.
