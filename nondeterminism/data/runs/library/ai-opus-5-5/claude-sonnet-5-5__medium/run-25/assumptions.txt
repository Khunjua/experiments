- A call with a valid date (and an object argument) that is not earlier than the current date advances the clock and runs hold expiry, even if other fields (ids, amount) are invalid or the business rules fail.
- A call whose argument is not an object, or whose date is malformed, changes nothing, including the clock.
- addBook and addMember are not dated: they do not touch the clock or expiry.
- Date comparisons use string comparison on validated dates, which orders them correctly.
- When a set-aside book is borrowed by its holder, the remaining holds stay waiting in order on the now on-loan book.
- When a hold expires, the member's active hold is removed, freeing a slot against the 5-hold limit.
- A member may place a hold on a book set aside for another member, but not on one set aside for themselves (duplicate hold).
- A non-integer or non-number `amount` fails payFine, as does an `amount` of 0 or less.
- Overdue for borrowing is checked against each loan's current due date, so renewals count.
