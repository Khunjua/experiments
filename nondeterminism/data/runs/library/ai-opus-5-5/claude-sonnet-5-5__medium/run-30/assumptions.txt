- A call whose argument is not an object, or whose date is invalid, does not touch the clock. A call with a valid, non-earlier date advances the clock and runs hold expiry even if its other fields are invalid or a business rule fails.
- Dates are compared as strings once validated, since the fixed-width format sorts the same as calendar order. Date arithmetic uses UTC day numbers.
- Adding days past year 9999 is not guarded. The result is formatted without error.
- `payFine` checks the amount type and sign after the member lookup. All failures return the same `{ ok: false }` plus a reason, so the order does not matter.
- A member's holds list keeps placement order. A hold is removed when it is fulfilled by borrowing or when it expires.
- `readyUntil` in `getMember` is non-null only when the member's hold is at position 1 and the book is not on loan.
- A member who borrows a book set aside for them has that hold fulfilled only after all borrow checks pass.
- Fine amounts and the owed balance are plain numbers. The result of `payFine` on a failed call includes no `owed` field.
