- A call with a valid date that is not earlier than the current date advances the clock and runs hold expiry, even if other arguments (ids, amount) are invalid or the call fails for a business reason.
- If the arguments object is missing or the date is invalid, the clock is not touched and no expiry runs.
- The hold queue is the source of truth for a set-aside book: a book is set aside when it has no loan and a non-empty queue, and its `readyUntil` is stored per book.
- A hold's `readyUntil` is reported only when it is at position 1 of a book that is not on loan.
- Active holds are counted by scanning all books for the member's queue entries.
- `getMember` orders holds by a global placement sequence number, which gives the oldest-first order.
- `payFine` requires `amount` to be a safe integer greater than 0, and fails when `amount` exceeds `owed`.
- Failures include a short `reason` string.
- The overdue check on borrow uses strict `dueDate < date`, as specified.
- Dates are stored internally as UTC day numbers and formatted back to zero-padded `YYYY-MM-DD`. Years 0001–9999 are handled with `setUTCFullYear` so that years below 100 are not shifted to 19xx.
