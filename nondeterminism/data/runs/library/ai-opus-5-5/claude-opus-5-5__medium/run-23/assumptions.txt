- The clock advances and hold expiry runs only when every argument has a valid type and format. Calls with malformed input change nothing, not even the current date. Failures for business reasons, including an unregistered member or book, still advance the clock.
- A date earlier than the current date causes failure before any expiry processing, and nothing changes.
- `addBook` and `addMember` take no date. They do not touch the clock or run expiry.
- Date arithmetic uses a pure integer proleptic-Gregorian day count, which is equivalent to UTC. Date.UTC is not used, so years 0001–0099 are handled correctly.
- Due dates or readyUntil dates past 9999-12-31 are formatted as-is, with a year of 5 or more digits. They are not rejected.
- getMember returns `ok: true` together with loans, owed and holds.
- Renewal sets the due date to the later of the old due date and date + 21, as a safeguard. Per the spec, date + 21 is never earlier anyway.
- Hold expiry also removes the expired hold from the member's active hold list. Expired holds are not reported anywhere.
- When a set-aside book is borrowed by its ready member, the remaining holds wait with no readyUntil, because the book is now on loan.
- A member who already has this book on loan cannot place a hold on it. A member whose hold is ready on a set-aside book cannot place a second hold on it, because that counts as a duplicate.
- If a getter or other exotic input throws, the method catches the error and returns `{ ok: false }`.
- Failure results include a `reason` string.
- `payFine` with a non-integer, non-safe-integer or non-positive amount is invalid input and does not advance the clock.
