- A call with a missing argument object, a missing or empty identifier, a malformed or non-existent calendar date, or an invalid payment amount is treated as invalid input: it returns { ok: false } without advancing the clock or running hold expiry.
- A call with a valid date that fails a business rule still advances the current date and runs hold expiry, as required by §3.3.
- A dated call whose date is earlier than the current date fails before any clock or expiry processing.
- getMember with an unregistered member but a valid date still advances the clock and runs hold expiry, then returns { ok: false }.
- Dates are stored internally as whole-day integers; every output date is formatted as YYYY-MM-DD with the year zero-padded to four digits.
- No check is made for results beyond 9999-12-31; such dates are formatted with more than four year digits.
- A book's readyUntil is null whenever the book is on loan or has no ready hold.
- Active holds are counted across all books' queues, including ready holds.
- getMember orders holds by global placement sequence, so holds on different books are interleaved by when they were placed.
- A ready hold blocked by a member's loan limit, overdue loan, or debt stays in place and keeps the book set aside, as §6.1 requires.
- A loan is overdue for borrowing when its due date is before the borrow date, and for renewing when the renewal date is after its due date; a loan due on the same day is not overdue.
- Returning a book with waiting holds sets readyUntil to the return date plus 7, even if the returned copy was late.
