- Unknown memberId or bookId makes borrow, returnBook, renew, placeHold, and payFine return { ok: false }.
- getMember for an unknown member returns { loans: [], owed: 0, holds: [] }.
- addBook and addMember return { ok: false } when the id already exists.
- Ids are used as Map keys with strict equality; no type or format validation is done on ids or dates.
- The set-aside member stays at position 1 in the line until they borrow the book or their hold expires, and is counted in positions.
- Set-aside expiry is applied lazily at the start of every call that has a date, and also in getMember.
- A set-aside is valid through readyUntil inclusive and expires on the day after readyUntil.
- When a set-aside expires and the next member is set aside, the new readyUntil is the old readyUntil + 3 days; if that is also already past, the process repeats.
- An expired hold is removed from that member's holds list.
- A book set aside for one member cannot be borrowed by anyone else, including members who hold it later in the line.
- A set-aside member who borrows the book is subject to all normal borrow limits (5 loans, owed under 1000 cents); if they fail a limit, the hold stays in place.
- Borrowing a set-aside book removes that member from the front of the line; the book then stays on loan with the remaining members still queued.
- Renewal is refused whenever the hold line for the book is non-empty, including when a book is set aside.
- Renewal does not check the member's owed amount; a member who owes money can still renew.
- Renewal of a loan is allowed only on or before the due date; there is no separate check for the loan being returned or missing.
- A return with lateDays of 3 or more is charged 25 cents per late day from the first day, capped at 500 cents, and the fine is the total days times 25.
- Returning a book that has no holds leaves it free on the shelf with no set-aside.
- Placing a hold is refused only for a book that is free on the shelf, a book the member has on loan, or a book the member already holds.
- There is no limit on how many holds one member may have.
- A hold may be placed on a book that is set aside for another member.
- payFine rejects non-integer amounts, amounts of 0 or less, and amounts greater than the amount owed.
- getMember returns holds in the order the member placed them, and loans in insertion order.
- Renewal and borrow both use the date passed in the call as the transaction date; getMember's date also advances state.
- The loan object is shared between the book and the member, so renewals update both views.
