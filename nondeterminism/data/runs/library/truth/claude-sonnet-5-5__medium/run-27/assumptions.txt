- Hold expiry is applied lazily at the start of each method call that has a date, using that call's date. A hold is dropped only when readyUntil is strictly earlier than the call date.
- The member set aside for a book stays in the hold queue at position 1 and is counted in positions.
- Setting a book aside happens on return only if the queue is non-empty.
- Holds queued behind the member who borrows stay on hold while the book is on loan.
- addBook and addMember with an existing id are refused.
- Unknown members or books make every operation return ok:false.
- getMember for an unknown member returns empty loans and holds with owed 0.
- Renewal is refused if the book has any hold, including the one at position 1.
- A fine is charged only on return. Late days are counted from the due date as it stands after renewals.
- Fine of 3+ days late is 25 × late days, capped at 500.
- payFine requires an integer amount greater than 0 and no more than owed.
- A member may place a hold on a book set aside for another member, but not if already in its queue.
- A member whose hold is set aside for them can still be refused borrowing for the usual reasons (limit, owing 1000 or more). The hold then expires normally.
- addBook and addMember have no date, so they do not trigger expiry.
