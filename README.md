# experiments

Experiments on AI coding agents and the text they are given to work from. Each folder is one
experiment, frozen on the day its write-up came out: the code, the inputs, the hidden answers,
and everything the runs produced, so the numbers can be checked and the runs repeated.

| Date | Experiment | Question | Write-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-03 | [nondeterminism](nondeterminism/) | When the same text gives different programs, who is nondeterministic: the model, or the human who left decisions open? 270 programs from 3 texts and 3 models. | [dev.to](https://dev.to/entalpa/who-is-nondeterministic-the-model-or-the-human-1o7c) |

Charts are signed Entalpa Lab, the research side of [Entalpa](https://entalpa.com), a
spec-driven development platform for AI coding agents.
